# Collated name and logo use

The Collated name and logos identify Collated and the people who make it. The code in this project is open source; the brand is not (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This page says what you may do with the name and logos without asking.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to talk about Collated: in an article, a review, a talk, a tutorial or a list of tools.
- Say in plain words that your project works with, is built on, or is a plugin or integration for Collated, as long as it is clear that Matt Black did not make it.
- Use the unmodified logo to link to or point at Collated, following the brand guidelines (clear space, colours, minimum size).
- Keep these files unchanged in a copy or fork of the project, as long as the fork does not present itself as Collated (below).

## You may not

- Release, host or sell a fork, product or service under the Collated name or logo, or under a name or logo close enough to be mistaken for it. A fork that others use needs its own name and logo.
- Change the logos: redraw, stretch, recolour outside the guidelines, add to them, or combine them with your own marks.
- Use the name or logos in a way that suggests Matt Black made, sponsors or endorses your work when they don't, or as your own app icon, avatar or domain name.

## Anything else

Ask first: https://github.com/mtblck/collated/issues. Merchandise, events and anything not listed above need permission.
