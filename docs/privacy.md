# Privacy notice

_Last updated 21 October 2026._

Collated is a free, open-source tool. It is built so that your documents never leave your device, and this notice explains exactly what that means.

## Your documents

The files you add, the pages you arrange and the PDFs you export are processed only in your web browser. They are never uploaded, and nobody, including the author, can see them. When you close the tab they're gone, unless you saved the PDF.

## What is stored on your device

- **Your settings** (page setup, export quality, the searchable-text and page-number choices) are saved in your browser's local storage under `collated:settings`, so they're there next time. Your title and password are never saved.
- **Your light or dark theme choice** (`mb-theme`).
- **Text-recognition data:** if you use searchable text, the English recognition data (about 3 MB) is cached in your browser's IndexedDB so it doesn't download again. It contains nothing about your documents.

You can clear all of this in your browser's site settings. There are no cookies.

## What the hosting sees

Collated is a set of static files served by Vercel, at mattblack.space. Like any website, the host receives standard request information when your browser fetches those files: your IP address, your browser's user agent, the time, and which file was requested (for example, the text-recognition data when you first use searchable text). This is used for running and securing the service, not for tracking you, and Collated adds no analytics or tracking of its own. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Links out

**Support Collated** goes to Ko-fi and **Source code** to GitHub. Those sites have their own privacy policies and only see you if you follow the link.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/mtblck/collated/issues). Collated is made by Matt Black.
