# Getting started

1. **Add your pages.** Drop images or PDFs anywhere on the page, choose **Choose files**, or paste from the clipboard. Phone photos come in the right way up.
2. **Put them in order.** Drag a page by its handle, or select it and use **Move earlier** / **Move later** in the panel on the right.
3. **Fix any page that needs it.** Select a page to rotate it, straighten it, scale it, crop it to the paper, or clean it up with a filter. See [Arranging pages](arranging-pages.md).
4. **Choose the paper.** **Page setup** in the toolbar sets the paper size, orientation and margins for the whole PDF. See [Page setup](page-setup.md).
5. **Export.** **Export** in the toolbar opens the options (title and filename, image quality, a size limit, searchable text, page numbers and a password); **Export PDF** at the bottom saves the file. See [Exporting](exporting.md).

Made a mistake? **Undo** (⌘Z or Ctrl+Z) steps back through everything, including removed pages.
