# Page setup

**Page setup**, at the left of the toolbar, applies to every page of the PDF. Its button shows the current setup, for example _US Letter · Auto · 0.5 in margin_.

- **Page size:** US Letter, US Legal, Tabloid, A3, A4, A5, a **Custom** size, or **Match image**, where each page is sized to its own image at the resolution you choose (PDF pages keep their own size). Collated starts with the paper and units usual where you are (Letter and inches in North America, A4 and millimetres elsewhere).
- **Orientation:** **Portrait**, **Landscape**, or **Auto**, where each page turns to suit its image.
- **Margin:** blank space around each image. The image never prints into it.
- **Units:** millimetres or inches, for the margin and custom sizes.

Collated remembers your page setup on this device for next time.
